## Design decisions

- Exactly four simulators per topic, including one Doodle: **56 total**, below the maximum of five per topic. Round variations stay within these cards.
- Concepts are shown before numerical checking. Models include arrays, coins, balances, clocks, route maps, solids and charts.
- Each expedition has eight introductory, eight exploratory and eight challenge rounds. Ranges/scaffolds change where appropriate; some spatial games retain the operation with fresh constructions. Extended play retains the challenge setting.
- Puzzles vary mathematical data and arrangements. Per-game recent problem/scene signatures reject recent duplicates when alternatives exist.
- Doodles check endpoints and the entire route. Tap points provides the same construction without requiring freehand motor control.
- Articles teach each game concept and connect to the chapter. This is original conceptual practice, not a reproduction of every printed exercise or an official answer key.

## Research method

Official NCERT chapter/preliminary PDFs, ePathshala’s Grade 4 Maths Mela listing and the SCERT Haryana textbook listing established the book and sequence. Official downloads were intermittent, so complete chapter text was also read from mirrored copies of the same NCERT PDFs (`files.supercop.in/ncert-books/2025/class4/demm1XX.pdf`).
