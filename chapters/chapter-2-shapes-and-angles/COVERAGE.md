## Reading and Doodle coverage

Each of the 16 topic IDs in the chapter map has a matching article, worked example, three explorations, two interactive checks, three FAQs, two adult activities, reflection and related-concept links in `learning.js`.

Guided Doodle activities cover polygon sides/closure (`loops`), copying angles (`tester`), angle families (`painter`), paper creases (`fold`), body angles (`yoga`), natural openings (`garden`), triangular braces (`towers`), clock hands (`clock`) and degree measurement (`degrees`). The original D game (`doodle`) adds unscored freehand comparison sketches after estimation. The remaining six games use their established Arcade interactions because fixed lengths, exact stick moves, marked-letter counting, ramp motion and carrom reflection are better represented by those controls.

All new Doodle activities stay within the requested chapter concepts. The reading material distinguishes a right angle from obtuse/straight openings, accounts for the moving hour hand, explains the special assumptions behind square-paper folds, and identifies which marked letter openings are counted.
