## Appropriate level and scope

- Observation, matching evidence, changing a model, sequencing a process, planning and explaining are emphasised. NCERT’s spinner chapter explicitly prioritises exploration over formal theoretical explanations at this age.
- Rounds introduce the controls and familiar cases before adding larger sets, longer constructions or more varied contexts where useful. Continued play revisits the same concept with new generated puzzles; it does not introduce advanced physics formulas or financial products.
- Every topic has **exactly four cards**. The internal variations remain within their named game, including travel-mode/map stages and sky-observation/mission rounds.
- Direct manipulation is combined with decisions: route movement, budgeting, habitat-kit construction, connecting a web, home assembly, a solar model, a varied plate, experimental tests, process sequencing, sky controls and ten checked drawing activities.
- NCERT’s five Rs use **Repurpose**, whereas the supplied table uses **Repair**. Both are taught, with this distinction explicit in the game, article and guide.
- Community repairs involving electricity or sharp materials are assigned to responsible adults. Natural plants are not automatically safe, and traditional repellent use is not presented as guaranteed protection. The supplied health/hygiene extensions stay at general educational level.
- The plate’s graphic regions are not serving-size instructions. Foods can contribute to more than one group, and variety is not a guarantee against disease.
- Habitat and regional examples represent selected relationships and stories; they are not universal rules. The Moon can appear in daylight, and its normal phases are not Earth’s shadow.
- This is an original curriculum-linked learning package, not a verbatim NCERT answer key or an official NCERT publication.
