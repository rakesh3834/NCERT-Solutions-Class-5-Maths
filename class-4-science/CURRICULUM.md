## Earlier mission design (retained for the other 17 games)

Research revisited on 21 September 2026. Reference: NCERT **Our Wondrous World**, Grade 4, The World Around Us (EVS), book code 0435, first edition March 2025. The downloaded official chapter PDFs are the 2026–27 reprint. All ten chapters were re-read for activities and teacher notes before selecting playable subtopics.

The old 40 short drills are replaced by **20 games, two per chapter**, with **83 connected stages**. Drawing is retained only where it records an observation, forms a functional design or captures a testable prediction. Scientific procedures can recur in extended practice, but successive stages within a mission change both the task and scene. Twenty-four multi-stage missions form an expedition; free play follows.

## Why these activities are feasible

- Chapters 1–2 provide decisions children recognise: using public services, coordinating a festival, caring for saplings, navigating landmarks, choosing a communication method and keeping track of pretend money. These become planning situations with follow-up needs, rather than a series of labels to match.
- Chapter 3 explicitly calls for observation of animal features, leaf features and connections in nature. Counting, sketching veins and following a lost food or shelter connection preserve that observational purpose. Taking wildlife home and collecting plants are not necessary.
- Chapter 4 connects local materials, model homes, solar electricity, natural colours and grain storage. Building, observing runoff, testing sunlight and inspecting a store offer visible causes and consequences. Cultural facts remain explanations rather than arbitrary speed trivia.
- Chapters 5–6 connect everyday meals, cooking, routines, cooperation and well-being. Tasks use a stated situation, acknowledge food-group overlap and avoid using plate areas as prescribed serving sizes. Emotional safety activities involve listening and trusted help; they do not diagnose a child.
- Chapter 7 emphasises exploration of spinners and boats without requiring formal theory. The strongest game loop is predict, test, change a feature, retest and explain. In Doodle Boat Lab the actual drawing dimensions affect a simplified cargo model. Spinner readings are illustrative comparisons, not real spin-time predictions.
- Chapter 8 asks for same-size paper samples and equal quantities in comparative observations. The game requires comparable conditions before using model property readings. Paper-making stages transform a material instead of merely asking for their names.
- Chapter 9 uses representative regional stories. The games distinguish observed landscape, practical adaptation, current weather and respectful descriptions; a postcard never claims that all residents live alike.
- Chapter 10 supports a shadow prediction followed by observation and changing the light height. Moon observations and Earth rotation remain separate models. Calendar intervals are approximate. Historical Chandrayaan events are a separate timeline, not an explanation of phases.

## Drawing decisions

1. **Nature Detective:** record parallel or branching veins from a visible leaf.
2. **Doodle Boat Lab:** draw an open hull whose measured width and depth influence the load test; diagnose and redesign.
3. **Shadow Sketch Lab:** draw a prediction, compare it with an observed model shadow, then investigate light height.

Decorative tracing of roots, generic triangles, plate partitions and arbitrary star zigzags was retired. Accessible controls construct the same scientific representation without requiring freehand movement.

## Coverage and boundaries

The supplied subtopics remain in the chapter's two games and their merged Read articles. Topics that are better explained than simulated—festivals, examples of traditional food, plant repellents, communication history, cultural stories and machine-paper production—are retained in contextual reading. Not every fact is turned into a separate game.

NCERT's five Rs are Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, **Repurpose**, Recycle. The requested **Repair** is included and distinguished from repurposing. Natural materials are not automatically safe; traditional repellents are not guaranteed protection. Models are explicitly simplified. Normal Moon phases are not Earth's shadow. Deserts are defined by scarce precipitation, not universal heat. Foods can contribute to more than one group.

This is original curriculum-linked content, not an official NCERT publication or a verbatim answer key. The established [GoKurious article structure](https://gokurious.com/simulators/moon-phases/) and existing fonts/styles are retained: before trying, exploration, explanations, worked example, two interactive checks, reflection, three FAQs, adult activities and related lessons.
